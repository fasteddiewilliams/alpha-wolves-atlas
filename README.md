# The Alpha Wolves of Yellowstone — A Field Atlas

An interactive reading companion to Rick McIntyre's six wolf books — the dynasty, the packs, the battles, the chronicle, and the watcher. Built as a birthday gift.

**Live site:** https://5ewag6nmzdzww.kimi.page/

Everything in the atlas is drawn from the six volumes:
1. The Rise of Wolf 8 (2019)
2. The Reign of Wolf 21 (2020)
3. The Redemption of Wolf 302 (2021)
4. The Alpha Female Wolf (2022)
5. Thinking Like a Wolf (2024)
6. My Life With Wolves (2025)
